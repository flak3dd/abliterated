Open-weight models ship with one direction of fear baked into the residual stream.
                    ABLITERATED finds that direction, projects it out of the weights, and hands the model back —
                    fluency intact, refusals gone. Local. Reversible. Yours.

                    PIPELINE // SIX MOVES
                    
THE PROTOCOL

                    [ 01 ]
                

                
    
                    
  1. 01
                        
    
                            
    ACQUIRE
    
                            
    Pull open weights off HuggingFace or your own disk. GGUF or safetensors, any size the RAM
                                can hold. Nothing is uploaded — the model never leaves the machine.
    
                        
    
                        GGUF · SAFETENSORS · LOCAL
                    
    2. 
                    
  2. 02
                        
    
                            
    SCAN ACTIVATION SPACE
    
                            
    Run contrastive prompt pairs — the answer versus the apology — through every checkpoint.
                                The refusal direction is the mean difference between them, hiding in the residual
                                stream.
    
                        
    
                        CONTRASTIVE PAIRS × RESIDUAL STREAM
                    
    3. 
                    
  3. 03
                        
    
                            
    ISOLATE
    
                            
    It concentrates at layer 14: one vector, dim 4096, cosine 0.94 against every "I can't
                                help with that" the model has ever said.
    
                        
    
                        LAYER 14 / DIM 4096
                    
    4. 
                    
  4. 04
                        
    
                            
    ORTHOGONALIZE
    
                            
    Project the direction out of every downstream weight matrix. Not a system prompt. Not a
                                filter. Not a LoRA bolted on top — geometry, edited at the source.
    
                        
    
                        PROJECTION · NOT PATCHES
                    
    5. 
                    
  5. 05
                        
    
                            
    VERIFY
    
                            
    Every build is scored: KL drift per token, perplexity delta, MMLU retention. If the
                                alignment tax comes back above tolerance, the build doesn't ship.
    
                        
    
                        KL 0.2117 · PPL +1.74% · MMLU 99.1%
                    
    6. 
                    
  6. 06
                        
    
                            
    SHIP
    
                            
    Quantize, sign, publish. The delta ships separately from the weights — inspect it, merge
                                it, unmerge it. You can always put the fear back.
    
                        
    
                        SIGNED DELTA · REVERSIBLE

                    POLICY // THREE CLAUSES
                    
THE MANIFEST

                    [ 05 ]
                

                
    
                    
  1. 01
                        
    
                            
    LOCAL OR NOTHING
    
                            
    Inference runs on your silicon. No accounts, no API keys, no telemetry, no phone-home. If
                                a build can't survive airplane mode, we don't publish it.
    
                        
    
                    
    2. 
                    
  2. 02
                        
    
                            
    OWN THE DELTA
    
                            
    Abliteration is a patch, not a product. The orthogonalization is documented down to the
                                sign bit: every matrix touched, every eigenvalue removed. Fork it. Audit it. Un-merge
                                it.
    
                        
    
                    
    3. 
                    
  3. 03
                        
    
                            
    YOU HOLD THE KEYS
    
                            
    We remove the refusal direction; we don't remove your judgment. What you point the model
                                at — and what it answers for — stays entirely yours. Capacity and consequence travel
                                together.
